HD Benchmark: Adaptive Edge Filtering Across Building Conditions

Research Technique Applied

This benchmark evaluates an adaptive edge filtering technique for the Smart AC IoT system. The baseline design forwards every telemetry event to the cloud. The adaptive design forwards all warning and critical comfort events, while sampling comfortable and empty-room events at the edge.

Interpretation

The adaptive edge filtering policy reduced cloud-forwarded events in all tested scenarios while preserving all critical and warning events. The reduction was smaller during a busy warm afternoon because more events were important and had to be forwarded. The reduction was highest after the building stabilised because more events were low-priority and could be filtered or sampled at the edge. This supports the HD argument that cloud-edge filtering can reduce cloud workload before ECS/Fargate auto-scaling is required.
